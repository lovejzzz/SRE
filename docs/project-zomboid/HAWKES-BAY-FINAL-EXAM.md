## Target and source baseline

Reconstruct Alma Reynard’s house from the Nightcall mission in HITMAN 2 / HITMAN World
of Assassination, including the surrounding beach and the original nighttime setting.
The user explicitly requests 1:1 fidelity, the entire interior, furnishings/props, and
the concealed room. The task is scene reconstruction in the editable Tian House
workshop. Assassination objectives and the original game's complete AI are not implied
by this architectural brief; environmental interactions needed to experience the house
and access its concealed room are part of the scene.

Before measurement, choose and record one actual game edition/build, graphics settings,
mission variant and mission state. Do not mix the normal Nightcall house with seasonal
remixes, unrelated real houses, or differently staged mission states. Prefer a playable
reference with systematic captures; supplement with maps, uninterrupted walkthroughs
and closeups. Whether a usable original installation is available is not established.

## Required reconstruction

| Area | What must be evidenced and reproduced |
|---|---|
| Site | House placement/orientation, coast and waterline, beach extent, sand profiles, dunes, planting, rocks and visible site objects. Fix the surveyed boundary explicitly; do not replace the beach with a generic yard. |
| Envelope | Full massing, every floor/roof/terrace, structural thickness, entrances, glazing, frames, stairs, railings, overhangs and exterior fixtures. |
| Interior | Room-by-room measured topology, circulation and all visible finishes. Inventory every room from the actual reference before declaring the list complete. |
| Furnishings | Furniture, appliances, lighting, artwork, curtains, shelves, contents and small props; dimensions, orientation, material and support relationships. Repeated objects share recipes; all remain editable. |
| Concealed room | Hidden entrance and its mechanism, opening motion, passage clearance, interior layout and all evidenced storage/equipment/contents. A labelled locked room or painted doorway does not satisfy this. |
| Night scene | Source-matched artificial lighting, sky/moon illumination, warm/cool balance, shadows, glass reflections, water appearance, exterior visibility and interior/exterior contrast. Do not substitute one global dark tint. |
| Experience | Reachable rooms, doors and stairs; appropriate object/environment interactions; route from beach through the house, concealed room and accessible roof/terrace. |

“1:1” remains a requirement, not an automatic success label. Track dimensional error,
missing objects, material/lighting mismatches and rendering limitations explicitly. The
current integer grid and fixed isometric renderer may prevent exact measurements or
matching source camera views; investigate and improve those capabilities rather than
quietly relaxing the target. Similar atmosphere or a recognisable silhouette is insufficient.

   Preserve original recipes → exact-lossless delivery atlas → semantic directory.
   For complex materials/props, retain parameters, scale anchors and tested examples.
5. Validate representative assemblies before multiplying variants: glazing corner,
   secret entrance, stair/landing, furnished room, dune/water edge and interior light
   viewed from the dark beach. Record failures and corrections.

## Examination protocol

- This house is a **known-reference capstone**, because research and tool preparation
  are expressly allowed. Preserve the separate frozen unseen-photo test in the design
  document; do not relabel this researched house as unseen.
- Before the scored attempt, freeze the application/command schemas, asset/recipe versions,
  input corpus, source edition/state, scene boundary and comparison checklist. Archive
  any exploratory house sketches separately from the scored attempt.
- Start a new editable scene and build only through the public workshop operations.
  Export the operation history and scene. If a capability failure requires an engine
  change, log the failed attempt, improve the general tool, re-freeze and rerun affected
  acceptance. Do not silently patch a frozen test or hide special-case assistance.
- Deliver source/result pairs for all surveyed exterior views, beach approaches, every
  room, the secret entrance and hidden room, roof/terrace, and night lighting conditions.
  Match camera parameters where supported; label isometric-only comparisons as insufficient
  to establish source-view pixel fidelity.
- Check topology and scale separately from appearance. Use inventory coverage and measured
  deviations; never derive a completion percentage from the number of passing unit tests.
- Actually walk every route and exercise relevant mechanisms, including opening and closing
  the concealed entrance. Check collisions, sight, lighting, repeated opening states and
  recoverability. Save/reload, export/import, undo/redo and further manual editing must work.
- Measure representative building, catalogue and runtime performance against the design
  document's budgets. Preserve all existing houses and user saves.
- Present remaining differences and the comparison evidence for human visual review.
  Do not declare exact fidelity from self-assessment alone. Deploy the accepted result to
  tian.house and verify that the published scene/assets match the accepted build.

Deliverables: surveyed reference set and provenance, per-room/site inventory, scale and
uncertainty ledger, reusable tool/recipe improvements, editable final save, command history,
source/result comparisons, route/interaction reports, persistence/performance evidence,
remaining-difference report and verified deployed version.
